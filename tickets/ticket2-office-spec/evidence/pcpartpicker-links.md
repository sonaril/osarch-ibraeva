# PCPartPicker Compatibility & Power Verification

> **Note on currency:** PCPartPicker does not list prices in Kazakhstani Tenge (KZT) or local Kazakhstan retailers[cite: 14, 15, 42]. These four build lists are used strictly to verify **hardware compatibility** (CPU socket, DDR4 memory support, M.2 NVMe slot, GPU clearance) and **estimated total wattage** against each selected power supply[cite: 14, 15, 42]. All KZT prices are documented separately in [`../budget.csv`](../budget.csv) and [`vendor-quotes/`](vendor-quotes/)[cite: 14, 15].

- **Build 1 — Accountant:** https://pcpartpicker.com/list/DL8CMF — Verified LGA1700 + DDR4 + M.2 compatibility and estimated load against the 450W PSU[cite: 14, 15, 20, 21, 22, 26, 28, 29].
- **Build 2 — Sales Manager:** https://pcpartpicker.com/list/BhC9R4 — Verified AM4 + DDR4 + M.2 compatibility and ~174W estimated load against the 450W PSU (note PCPartPicker's BIOS/compatibility warning for Ryzen 5000G on A520 boards)[cite: 7, 14, 15, 22, 26, 29, 30, 31, 32, 42].
- **Build 3 — Web Developer:** https://pcpartpicker.com/list/kZhZqd — Verified LGA1700 + DDR4 + M.2 + Micro-ATX form-factor compatibility and estimated load against the 450W PSU[cite: 14, 15, 20, 22, 29, 32, 35, 36, 37].
- **Build 4 — Designer:** https://pcpartpicker.com/list/HvPGdq — Verified LGA1700 + DDR4 + M.2 compatibility, RTX 3060 12GB clearance inside the Lian Li A3-mATX case, and estimated load against the 600W PSU[cite: 14, 15, 20, 23, 26, 29, 32, 35, 38, 39, 40].
